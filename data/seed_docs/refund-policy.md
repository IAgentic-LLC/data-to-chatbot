# Refund and Returns Policy
Owner: Customer Experience (cx-policy@dropline.example). Last reviewed: 2026-04-01.

## Standard returns
Regular catalogue items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unworn, in the original box, with tags attached. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return being scanned at our warehouse.

## Limited drops
Items bought in a limited drop (any release marked "Drop" on the product page, for example the Friday 10:00 releases) have a 7-day return window from delivery. This rule was introduced by policy change PC-12, effective 2026-04-01, after incident INC-2047. PC-12 applies to every drop released on or after 2026-04-01. Before PC-12, drops used the standard 30-day window. The length of the drop window depends on the customer's delivery address, so check the sections for EU customers and for UK and US customers below before answering.

## EU customers
Customers with an EU delivery address always keep the legal 14-day right of withdrawal, including for limited drops. For EU customers the effective drop window is therefore 14 days, not 7.

## UK and US customers
UK and US customers follow the 7-day drop window and the 30-day standard window above.

## Exceptions
Defective items can be returned within 90 days regardless of drop status. Support agents can approve one goodwill exception per customer per year with a team lead.
