# Shipping Policy
Owner: Logistics.

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days in the EU and the UK, and 2 to 4 business days in the US. Express delivery (1 to 2 business days) is available in all three regions for 12 EUR, 10 GBP or 14 USD.

## Where we ship
We ship to EU countries, the UK and the US. We do not ship to other countries yet.

## Drop orders
Drop orders ship within 48 hours of the drop. Customers cannot change the delivery address after a drop order is placed.
