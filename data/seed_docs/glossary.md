# Dropline Glossary

## Terms
Drop: a limited release of a product at a fixed time, usually Friday 10:00, with a fixed number of pairs.
Waiting room: a queue page that admits customers to a drop in batches.
PC: policy change. Policy changes are numbered, for example PC-12.
INC: incident. Incidents are numbered, for example INC-2047.
Drop Club: Dropline's free loyalty program.
