# On-Call and Escalation
Owner: Engineering management.

## Rotation
Platform and Payments each run a weekly on-call rotation that starts on Monday at 09:00 CET.

## Severity levels
SEV-1: customers cannot buy, for example checkout is down during a drop. Page immediately, respond within 5 minutes.
SEV-2: a major feature is degraded. Respond within 30 minutes.
SEV-3: limited impact. Handle in working hours.

## Escalation
If the on-call engineer does not respond within 10 minutes, the pager escalates to the team lead, then to the head of engineering.
