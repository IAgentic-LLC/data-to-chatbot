# Postmortem INC-2047: Reseller return wave after the Air Glide drop
Date of drop: 2026-03-13 (Friday, 10:00). Severity: SEV-3. Owner: CX and Fraud.

## What happened
The Air Glide drop sold 5,000 pairs in 4 minutes. Over the next 30 days, 1,180 pairs were returned. 41% of those returns came from 12 accounts linked to resellers, who bought in bulk and returned unsold pairs near the end of the 30-day window.

## Impact
Returned pairs could not be resold as new stock for the drop, and genuine customers missed out.

## Actions
1. Shorten the return window for limited drops (became policy change PC-12, 7 days, effective 2026-04-01).
2. Limit drops to 1 pair per customer per release.
3. Fraud team reviews accounts with more than 3 drop returns in 90 days.
