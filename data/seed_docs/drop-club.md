# Drop Club Loyalty Program
Owner: Growth.

## Membership
Drop Club is free. Members earn 1 point per euro spent (or per pound or dollar in the UK and US).

## Benefits
Members get early access to every limited drop, 10 minutes before the public release (09:50 instead of 10:00). At 500 points members get free express delivery for a year.

## Points on returns
Points earned on a returned order are removed when the refund is issued.
